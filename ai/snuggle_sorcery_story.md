# Snuggle Sorcery: The Academy of Gentle Curses

*A cozy adventure game story*

---

## The World

Every feeling leaves a little something behind. Big scary feelings once made big scary curses, but in the misty harbour city of **Lantern Bay**, something changed. Ever since the Great Warm Winter, the curses born from people's small worries have come out… *fluffy*.

They're called **Grumblings**. A Grumbling born from "I forgot my umbrella" is a soggy cloud-puff that rains on your shoes. One born from "nobody picked me for their team" is a lonely pom-pom that follows you around, sighing. They aren't evil. They're just feelings that never got a hug.

Sorcerers used to *exorcise* curses. At **Mistbloom Academy of Gentle Sorcery**, students learn something harder: how to *soothe* them.

---

## Core Mechanic (Story-Linked)

Every sorcerer carries **Cozy Energy**, a warm glow that builds up when you do kind things: sharing snacks, listening, tucking someone in. Students channel it through their personal **Soothing Technique**. When a Grumbling is fully calmed, it doesn't vanish. It turns into a tiny **Charm Sprite** that joins your team, remembering the feeling that made it and helping others with that same feeling.

The most powerful sorcerers can unfold a **Domain of Comfort**: a pocket world built from what makes them feel safe, where every Grumbling inside is guaranteed a hug.

---

## The Main Cast

**Xiao Pei (the player character)**
A first-year who arrives at Mistbloom with a cardboard suitcase and no idea she's a sorcerer. She discovers her gift when a soggy Grumbling falls asleep in her lap on the train. Her Soothing Technique is **Lullaby Thread**: she hums, and glowing yarn wraps Grumblings into snug little cocoons.

**Doudou**
Xiao Pei's companion, a round, sleepy Grumbling born from the feeling of "five more minutes." Shaped like a steamed bun with stubby ears, he lives in her hood and wakes up only for dumplings and emergencies. Secretly, he is much older than he looks.

**Lin Tangtang**
A bubbly second-year and the best baker at the Academy. Her technique, **Sugarcraft**, lets her bake pastries infused with Cozy Energy; one bite and any Grumbling cheers up. She's loud, bossy, and will absolutely adopt you as her little sibling.

**Wei Bao**
A shy boy who speaks mostly through his hand puppet, a felt goose named Captain Honk. His technique, **Echo Friend**, lets Grumblings speak through the puppet, so he can finally hear what they're upset about. He understands them better than anyone because he used to be lonely too.

**Master Fang Qiuyue**
The Academy's teacher, a gentle old woman in an enormous knitted cardigan with pockets full of sweets. She's rumoured to be the strongest sorcerer in Lantern Bay, but no one has ever seen her fight. Her Domain of Comfort, **Grandmother's Kitchen**, has only been opened once, fifty years ago.

---

## Prologue: The Rainy Train

Xiao Pei is travelling to live with her aunt in Lantern Bay. On the train, a small grey cloud begins raining on every passenger's shoes. Everyone grumbles and moves away. Xiao Pei, feeling sorry for it, hums the lullaby her mother used to sing.

The cloud stops raining. It curls up in her lap and snores.

When the train arrives, Lin Tangtang is waiting on the platform with a sign reading "WELCOME NEW STUDENT (probably)" and a box of custard tarts. "You're a sorcerer," she announces. "Also you have a cloud on you."

*Tutorial: soothing your first Grumbling; meeting Doudou, who has been napping in Xiao Pei's hood the whole time.*

---

## Chapter 1: Welcome to Mistbloom

Mistbloom Academy sits on a hill above the harbour, all sloping tiled roofs, paper lanterns, and a courtyard full of Charm Sprites playing tag. Master Fang welcomes Xiao Pei with a warm hand on her head and a lemon candy.

Xiao Pei learns the basics: Grumblings grow bigger when they're ignored, and smaller when someone notices them. Her first missions are small. A lost-sock Grumbling in the laundry room. A "didn't finish my homework" Grumbling hiding under the library stairs.

She meets Wei Bao when Captain Honk interrupts her lesson to announce, "THE NEW GIRL HAS CRUMBS ON HER FACE." Wei Bao hides behind his puppet in mortification. They become friends immediately.

*Gameplay: Academy hub, first Soothing missions, befriending classmates, cooking with Tangtang to build Cozy Energy.*

---

## Chapter 2: The Night Market Mix-Up

The team visits Lantern Bay's night market, where a flock of "I want that but can't afford it" Grumblings has been knocking over stalls. They look like tiny round sparrows with enormous wistful eyes.

The trick, Xiao Pei discovers, isn't buying them things. It's sitting with them and pointing out all the free good things too: the smell of roasting chestnuts, the lanterns on the water, the street musician's song. One by one they chirp happily and become Charm Sprites that guide lost children back to their parents.

That night, walking home, the friends notice something strange. All the lanterns on the far side of the harbour have gone out, and the air there feels cold and heavy.

*Gameplay: first multi-Grumbling encounter, team-up Soothing combos, market mini-games.*

---

## Chapter 3: The Quiet District

Grumblings across the city are changing. They're getting grey. Heavy. They don't want to be hugged anymore; they just want to be left alone. And they're all drifting toward the Quiet District, an old neighbourhood of shuttered shops across the harbour.

Wei Bao uses Echo Friend on one of the grey Grumblings, and Captain Honk speaks in a tiny, sad voice: *"Nobody remembers us. So we're going to where nobody remembers anything."*

Master Fang goes very still when she hears this. That evening, she tells the students a story. Fifty years ago, a Grumbling grew so big from everyone's forgotten feelings that it covered half the city in a grey fog. She calmed it once, but only barely, and only because she had help. She had assumed it was gone for good.

"It isn't a monster," she says. "It's everyone's loneliness, all rolled together. And loneliness always comes back when people stop checking on each other."

*Gameplay: investigation chapter, exploring the Quiet District, uncovering the old story through Charm Sprite memories.*

---

## Chapter 4: Doudou's Secret

In the Quiet District, the fog grows so thick that Xiao Pei is separated from her friends. Alone and scared, she finds that Doudou is wide awake for the first time.

He tells her the truth. Fifty years ago, he was one of the Grumblings that made up the great fog, a tiny "five more minutes" feeling from a little girl who didn't want to leave her grandmother's kitchen. That girl was Master Fang. When she calmed the fog, one small piece clung to her and became Doudou. He's been sleeping in sorcerers' pockets and hoods ever since, waiting for someone who could finish what she started.

"You don't just soothe feelings," he tells Xiao Pei. "You *stitch* them back to people. That's what your thread does."

Xiao Pei's Lullaby Thread glows brighter than ever, and she follows it back to her friends.

*Gameplay: solo stealth-and-comfort section, emotional turning point, Lullaby Thread upgrade.*

---

## Chapter 5: The Great Sulk

At the heart of the Quiet District, the friends find it: **the Great Sulk**, a Grumbling the size of a building, shaped like a huge grey blanket-lump with just two enormous tearful eyes peeking out. Every forgotten birthday, unanswered letter, and "I'm fine, really" in Lantern Bay has piled into it.

It doesn't attack. It just sighs, and the sigh makes everyone who hears it want to curl up alone and give up.

The final encounter unfolds in phases:

1. **Tangtang** bakes the biggest cake anyone has ever seen, right there on the street, and the smell cuts through the fog.
2. **Wei Bao** lets the Great Sulk speak through Captain Honk. It lists every forgotten feeling it carries, and Wei Bao answers each one: "I hear you. That sounds really hard."
3. **Every Charm Sprite** the player has befriended throughout the game returns, each one comforting the piece of the Great Sulk that matches the feeling it was born from.
4. **Master Fang** arrives, finally, and opens her Domain of Comfort for the second time in fifty years. The street transforms into Grandmother's Kitchen: steaming pots, a crackling stove, a big wooden table with room for everyone.
5. **Xiao Pei** unfolds her own Domain of Comfort for the first time: **The Everyone Blanket**, an enormous patchwork quilt stitched from glowing thread, big enough to wrap the whole Great Sulk and everyone in it.

And under the blanket, in the warm kitchen, the Great Sulk does something it has never done before. It falls asleep.

*Gameplay: multi-phase finale drawing on every relationship and Charm Sprite collected.*

---

## Epilogue: Morning in Lantern Bay

The fog lifts. The Great Sulk has shrunk into thousands of tiny, glowing Charm Sprites that float back across the harbour to the people they came from, each one a small reminder to call a friend, write back, or say "actually, I'm not fine, can we talk?"

Lanterns relight all across the Quiet District. Shops reopen. Neighbours who hadn't spoken in years share breakfast in the street.

Back at Mistbloom, Master Fang gives Xiao Pei a hand-knitted cardigan, just like hers, with extra-deep pockets "for Grumblings and sweets." Tangtang declares a week-long party. Captain Honk gives a speech. Wei Bao, for the first time, gives one too, without the puppet.

And Doudou, finally finished with the job he waited fifty years to do, climbs into Xiao Pei's hood, curls up, and says:

*"Five more minutes."*

**THE END**

*…but new Grumblings are always being born. Post-game: free-roam Lantern Bay, collect every Charm Sprite, and help the Academy welcome next year's first-years.*
